Six short questions about seeding the Fork P2P swarm and about how Fork should be priced. It takes about a minute.

03How much space are you willing to donate to seed the Fork P2P swarm?
What "donate" means: you set aside part of your disk for the Fork network. It stores small encrypted pieces of other users' projects, so their music stays available when their computer is off. Nobody, not even Fork, can open those pieces, and they never touch your own files. It only works while your computer is on, it pauses on battery, and you can change the amount at any time in Settings.
04Is Fork Free a good offer?
The offer: free access to the studio tools, plus unlimited direct P2P, as long as you keep your computer on to share.
05Is €2.99/month a good price for Pro?
The offer: 10 GB of P2P Cloud for high availability, without keeping your PC on.
