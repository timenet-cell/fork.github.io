Every licence Fork issues is a compressed NFT in a single Fork collection on Solana. The NFT is the licence: whoever holds it holds the listening rights and the right to resell it where the terms allow.

Each edition for sale has its own page here, at forkgate.io/r/<edition id>. It shows the signed licence terms and a fingerprint of them, which your browser recomputes from the terms it receives, so you can see that they match the link or QR code you followed.

Publishing on Fork costs creators nothing, and this registry is kept from Fork's own records, without querying the chain. Who owns a particular licence is shown only to its owner; if a licence moved to another wallet, its page links to that asset on Solana Explorer.
