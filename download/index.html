Opening it the first time

Fork Studio isn’t notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm the first time you open it. You only do this once. After that it opens like any other app, and updates arrive inside the app.

  1. Move it to Applications. Open the downloaded zip and drag Fork Studio into your Applications folder.
  2. Try to open it. macOS says it can’t verify the app. That’s expected. Click Done.
  3. Allow it. Go to System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll to the Security section and click Open Anyway next to Fork Studio.
  4. Confirm. Click Open Anyway once more and enter your password or use Touch ID.

Don’t see Open Anyway? It shows for about an hour after the first attempt. Open Fork Studio again, then go back to Privacy & Security.

Prefer Terminal? This clears the downloaded-from-the-internet flag:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Fork Studio.app"
