Fork - Support, refunds and delivery

Contact

Email support@forkgate.io. We answer within 5 working days. If something is wrong with a purchase, please write to us before opening a dispute with your bank: we can usually fix it faster.

What you buy

Music on Fork is sold by the artist; Fork is the platform. Each purchase is a digital licence to a track, delivered to your Fork app right after payment. Payments are processed by Stripe.

Delivery

Delivery starts as soon as the payment is confirmed: the licence appears in your Library and you can listen to it in the app straight away.

Returns and refunds

  • A new track can be returned within 14 days of payment, from the Library in the app: "Problem with this purchase? Get a refund". It is instant, with no questions asked, and you give the licence back.
  • Listening in the app does not affect this. The return window ends for good the first time you put the licence up for sale, send it to your own wallet, or download the master.
  • The refund is the price of the track. The gas cost and the service fee shown at checkout are not refundable.
  • A purchase paid with your Fork balance is refunded to your balance; a card payment goes back to the card.
  • Used tracks are final sale. Balance you add is credited right away and is non-refundable.
  • One return per licence. For anything outside these cases, write to support@forkgate.io.

Cancellations

There are no subscriptions in the store: each purchase is a one-off payment, so there is nothing to cancel after it.
